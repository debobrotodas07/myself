02 / What I make

Good conversations
deserve a second life.

For the past three years, I’ve been learning the craft of podcast clipping: watching long conversations closely, spotting moments worth sharing, then shaping them into short-form videos for YouTube and TikTok.

I’ve experimented with multiple channels along the way. The results have taken time, but every attempt has taught me more about patience, consistency, storytelling, and getting a little better with each edit.

This is a journey in progress. I share the experience honestly, and let the work grow at its own pace.

03 / The creative rhythm

Find the moment.
Shape the story.

A simple way to think about the craft behind a short, memorable clip.

STEP 01◌

Listen closely

Start with the whole conversation. Notice the shift in energy, the surprising thought, the moment that makes you lean in.

Attention before action
STEP 02✳

Find the story

Look for a clear idea with feeling behind it — something that can stand on its own and invite people into the wider conversation.

A moment with meaning
STEP 03↗

Shape the clip

Use pacing, editing, and storytelling to give that moment a focused, engaging form for a short-form audience.

Edit with intention
